02 About

Bangkok → Munich

I like starting where things are unclear: new products, unfamiliar domains, many teams with different goals.

I'm a product designer originally from Thailand, now based in Munich. I've designed for robot engineers, content creators, road operators and finance teams — usually at an early stage, when the product still has to figure out what it is.

My job isn't just understanding users. It's also understanding my colleagues: getting engineers, PMs and business teams to see the same picture, so we build the right thing faster. I'm observant by nature and tend to spot the small process flaws that cause big problems.
